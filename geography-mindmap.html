🌋 Physical Geography

🌍 Earth Structure

Crust → Mantle → Core. Study seismic waves, Earth's interior, continental drift and plate tectonics.

🪨 Rocks

Igneous, sedimentary and metamorphic rocks. Understand rock cycle and economic importance.

🏔️ Landforms

Mountains, plateaus, plains, valleys, folds, faults, erosion and deposition.

Aspect Details
Geomorphology Study of landforms and processes shaping Earth's surface.
Weathering Physical, chemical and biological breakdown of rocks.
Erosion Removal and transportation of material by agents such as water, wind and ice.
Plate Tectonics Movement and interaction of lithospheric plates.
🇮🇳 Indian Physiography

🏔️ Himalayas

Young fold mountains with major ranges: Himadri, Himachal and Shiwalik.

🌾 Northern Plains

Formed mainly by alluvial deposits of Indus, Ganga and Brahmaputra systems.

⛰️ Peninsular Plateau

Ancient stable landmass containing major mineral resources.

🏜️ Indian Desert

Arid region dominated by low rainfall, sand dunes and seasonal drainage.

🌊 Coastal Plains

Western and Eastern Coastal Plains have distinct widths, rivers and coastal features.

🏝️ Islands

Andaman & Nicobar and Lakshadweep have different geological and ecological characteristics.

🟢 Prelims Focus

Physiographic divisions, mountain passes, plateaus, coastal features, islands and important locations.

🟠 Mains Focus

Explain how physiography influences climate, drainage, agriculture, settlement and economic activity.

🌦️ Climate & Indian Monsoon
Aspect Details
Climate Controls Latitude, altitude, pressure and wind systems, distance from sea and relief.
Monsoon Seasonal reversal of winds with major importance for Indian rainfall.
Southwest Monsoon Main rainy season; Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal branches are important.
Western Disturbances Important weather systems affecting winter precipitation, especially northwestern India.
El Niño Pacific Ocean-atmosphere phenomenon that can influence global and Indian climate variability.

☀️ Hot Weather Season

High temperatures, local winds and pre-monsoon thunderstorms in several regions.

🌧️ Advancing Monsoon

Seasonal onset and spread of monsoon rainfall across India.

🍂 Retreating Monsoon

Withdrawal phase with important rainfall over parts of southeastern India.

🌊 Drainage System

🏔️ Himalayan Rivers

Generally perennial because they receive water from rainfall and snow/glacier sources.

🌊 Indus System

Includes the Indus and important tributaries such as Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas and Sutlej.

🌊 Ganga System

Major river system of northern India with numerous tributaries and extensive alluvial plains.

🌊 Brahmaputra

Flows through a major part of the Himalayan and northeastern drainage region.

🏞️ Peninsular Rivers

Many rivers have more seasonal flow and are associated with the older Peninsular landscape.

💧 Lakes

Study natural and artificial lakes, their locations and economic/ecological importance.

🌱 Indian Soils
Soil Major Features Important Areas
Alluvial Depositional soil; important for agriculture. Northern Plains and river valleys
Black Clay-rich; known for moisture retention. Deccan region
Red & Yellow Developed over crystalline rocks in many areas. Peninsular India
Laterite Strongly weathered under high rainfall and temperature conditions. Parts of Western Ghats and other tropical regions
Arid Low moisture and often sandy; salinity may occur locally. Arid and semi-arid regions
🌾 Agriculture

🌧️ Kharif

Crops generally associated with the monsoon season, including rice, maize and cotton.

❄️ Rabi

Winter-season crops such as wheat, gram and mustard.

☀️ Zaid

Short season between major agricultural seasons; watermelon and some vegetables are examples.

💧 Irrigation

Canals, wells, tube wells and other irrigation methods support agriculture in different regions.

🟢 Prelims

Crop-season matching, crop conditions, irrigation projects and agricultural regions.

🟠 Mains

Agricultural productivity, water stress, climate change, diversification and sustainable farming.

⛏️ Minerals & Energy Resources

⛏️ Coal

Important fossil fuel used for power generation and several industrial activities.

⛓️ Iron Ore

Major metallic mineral supporting the iron and steel industry.

🛢️ Petroleum

Important conventional energy resource with major onshore and offshore production regions.

☀️ Solar Energy

Important renewable energy source with high potential in several parts of India.

💨 Wind Energy

Strong potential in suitable coastal and high-wind regions.

💧 Hydropower

Uses flowing water for electricity generation; important in mountainous and river-rich areas.

🏭 Industries
Industry Key Factors
Iron & Steel Raw materials, energy, transport, water and markets.
Cotton Textile Raw material, labour, markets, power and transport.
Cement Limestone availability, energy, transport and market.
IT Industry Skilled workforce, connectivity, infrastructure and urban ecosystem.
🌳 Natural Vegetation & Wildlife

🌴 Tropical Evergreen

Dense forests associated with high temperature and high rainfall conditions.

🌳 Tropical Deciduous

Seasonal shedding of leaves; widely distributed in parts of India.

🌵 Thorn Forests

Adapted to low rainfall and dry conditions.

🏔️ Montane Forests

Vegetation changes with altitude in mountainous areas.

🌊 Mangroves

Salt-tolerant vegetation found especially in tidal and deltaic environments.

🐅 Wildlife

National parks, wildlife sanctuaries and biosphere reserves support biodiversity conservation.

⚠️ Natural Disasters

🌎 Earthquake

Sudden release of energy within Earth's crust. Focus on seismic zones and preparedness.

🌊 Flood

May result from intense rainfall, river overflow, storm surges and inadequate drainage.

🌪️ Cyclone

Strong atmospheric systems that can bring high winds, heavy rainfall and storm surge.

🏔️ Landslide

Slope failure influenced by rainfall, geology, slope conditions and human activity.

☀️ Drought

Prolonged water deficiency affecting agriculture, water supply and ecosystems.

🌊 Tsunami

Large sea waves commonly associated with major undersea disturbances.

🟢 Prelims

Causes, vulnerable regions, terminology, hazard characteristics and disaster locations.

🟠 Mains

Preparedness, mitigation, early warning, resilient infrastructure and community participation.

👥 Human Geography

👨‍👩‍👧 Population

Distribution, density, growth, demographic structure and population-resource relationship.

🚶 Migration

Movement of people due to economic, social, environmental and other factors.

🏙️ Urbanisation

Growth of towns and cities and associated infrastructure and governance challenges.

🏘️ Settlements

Rural and urban settlements vary according to physical and socio-economic conditions.

💼 Economic Geography

Agriculture, industry, transport, trade, services and spatial distribution of activities.

🌐 Globalisation

Increasing integration of economies, markets, technology and production networks.

🗺️ Map-Based UPSC Revision

🏔️ Mountains

Himalayas, Aravalli, Western Ghats, Eastern Ghats, Nilgiris and major passes.

🌊 Rivers

Indus, Ganga, Brahmaputra, Narmada, Tapi, Godavari, Krishna and Kaveri.

⚓ Ports

Learn major ports along the western and eastern coasts and their regional setting.

⛏️ Minerals

Important coal, iron ore, petroleum, natural gas and mineral belts.

🏞️ National Parks

Practice state-location matching of important national parks and protected areas.

🚧 Passes

Revise strategically important Himalayan and northeastern mountain passes.
