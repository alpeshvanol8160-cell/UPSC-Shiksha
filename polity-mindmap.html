🏛️ 1. Indian Constitution

The Constitution is the fundamental legal framework of India. For UPSC, focus on its structure, constitutional philosophy, important Parts, Articles, Schedules and amendments.

📊 Aspects → Details

Aspect Details
Constitution Making Constituent Assembly, constitutional debates and adoption.
Philosophy Sovereignty, democracy, republicanism, justice, liberty, equality and fraternity.
Preamble Provides a concise statement of constitutional objectives and values.
Fundamental Rights Part III of the Constitution.
DPSP Part IV of the Constitution.
Fundamental Duties Part IVA, Article 51A.
Amendment Article 368 provides the constitutional amendment procedure.

🎯 UPSC Focus

  • Preamble
  • Important Articles
  • Schedules
  • Constitutional Amendments
  • Basic Structure Doctrine

⚖️ 2. Fundamental Rights

Fundamental Rights are contained in Part III. Articles 12 and 13 provide the general framework, while Articles 14 onwards cover specific rights. 1

Right Main Area
Right to Equality Articles 14–18
Right to Freedom Articles 19–22
Right against Exploitation Articles 23–24
Freedom of Religion Articles 25–28
Cultural & Educational Rights Articles 29–30
Constitutional Remedies Article 32

📝 Quick Revision

Article 14
Equality before law and equal protection of laws.
Article 19
Six freedoms for citizens subject to constitutional restrictions.
Article 21
Protection of life and personal liberty.
Article 32
Constitutional remedies for enforcement of Fundamental Rights.

📜 3. DPSP & Fundamental Duties

Topic Constitutional Location Purpose
DPSP Part IV Guiding principles for governance.
Fundamental Duties Part IVA, Article 51A Civic responsibilities of citizens.

📌 Important Areas

📜 DPSP

Social and economic objectives of governance.

🇮🇳 Duties

Respect for Constitution, national symbols, environment and civic responsibility.

⚖️ FR vs DPSP

Understand their relationship and constitutional evolution.

👤 4. Union Executive

Institution Key Focus

Prime Minister Head of the Council of Ministers.

⚖️ 6. Judiciary

Topic Important Point

🇮🇳 7. Federalism

Indian federalism combines federal and unitary features. For UPSC, study Centre-State relations from legislative, administrative and financial perspectives.

Area Study Points
Legislative Relations Distribution of legislative powers.
Administrative Relations Coordination between Union and States.
Financial Relations Taxation, grants and resource distribution.
Inter-State Relations Mechanisms for cooperation and dispute resolution.

🚨 11. Emergency Provisions

Type Article Basic Area
