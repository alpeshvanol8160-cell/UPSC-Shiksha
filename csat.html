🧠 GENERAL STUDIES PAPER – II (CSAT)

Qualifying Paper • Objective Type

📌 Important

CSAT is a qualifying paper. Candidates must secure the minimum qualifying marks prescribed by UPSC.

1. 📖 Comprehension

Questions based on passages to test the candidate's ability to understand, interpret and analyse written information.

  • Reading comprehension
  • Main idea of a passage
  • Inference-based questions
  • Conclusion-based questions
  • Meaning and interpretation

2. 🗣️ Interpersonal Skills & Communication Skills

Questions relating to interpersonal skills and communication skills.

  • Communication ability
  • Understanding information
  • Interpersonal interaction
  • Effective communication

3. 🧩 Logical Reasoning & Analytical Ability

Questions designed to test logical thinking and analytical ability.

  • Logical reasoning
  • Statements and conclusions
  • Statements and assumptions
  • Arguments
  • Relationships
  • Series and patterns
  • Coding and decoding
  • Analytical puzzles

4. 🎯 Decision Making & Problem Solving

Questions relating to decision-making and problem-solving abilities.

  • Situation-based questions
  • Decision making
  • Problem solving
  • Practical reasoning
  • Choosing the most appropriate solution

5. 🧠 General Mental Ability

Questions testing general mental ability and basic reasoning skills.

  • Basic reasoning
  • Number patterns
  • Logical sequences
  • Analytical thinking
  • Problem-solving ability

6. 🔢 Basic Numeracy

Basic numeracy and data interpretation of Class X level.

  • Number system
  • Percentage
  • Ratio and proportion
  • Average
  • Profit and loss
  • Simple and compound interest
  • Time and work
  • Time, speed and distance
  • Mensuration
  • Basic algebra
  • Basic geometry

7. 📊 Data Interpretation

Data interpretation involving charts, graphs, tables and numerical information at approximately Class X level.

  • Tables
  • Bar graphs
  • Line graphs
  • Pie charts
  • Data comparison
  • Percentage-based data
  • Ratio-based data

8. 🇬🇧 English Language Comprehension Skills

Questions relating to English Language Comprehension skills at approximately Class X level.

  • Understanding English passages
  • Meaning of words in context
  • Understanding sentences
  • Comprehension-based questions

💡 Preparation Tip

Practice CSAT regularly. Focus on comprehension, reasoning, mathematics and data interpretation. Solve previous year questions to understand the actual level and pattern.
