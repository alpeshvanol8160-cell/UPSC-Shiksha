📋 Ethics Case Study Practice

Situation

You are a district-level public official. A contractor working on an important public project offers you a personal benefit in return for overlooking a minor procedural violation.

Questions

  1. What ethical issues are involved?
  2. What options are available to you?
  3. What would be your preferred course of action and why?
  4. How would you balance public interest with procedural fairness?

Suggested Approach

Identify stakeholders, ethical principles, competing values, possible options, consequences and safeguards. Your answer should demonstrate integrity, impartiality, accountability and commitment to public interest.
