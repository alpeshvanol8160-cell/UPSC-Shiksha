📚 GS-II Case Study Practice

Practice governance, constitutional and social-justice situations using a structured answer approach.

Case Study:

A district administration launches a welfare programme for vulnerable citizens. However, many eligible beneficiaries are unable to access the programme because of documentation, digital-access and awareness barriers.

  • Identify the major stakeholders.
  • Identify the governance challenges.
  • Suggest practical solutions.
  • Explain how technology can improve access without excluding citizens.
Suggested Approach:
  • Identify the constitutional and governance principles involved.
  • Consider inclusion and accessibility.
  • Use transparent and accountable procedures.
  • Provide both digital and offline access.
  • Use grievance-redressal mechanisms.

🎯 GS-II Preparation Strategy

  • Build strong concepts in Constitution, Polity, Governance, Social Justice and International Relations.
  • Connect static topics with current affairs.
  • Use constitutional articles, committees, judgments and examples where relevant.
  • Practice previous-year questions regularly.
  • Use headings, subheadings and balanced arguments in Mains answers.
  • For governance questions, include practical, citizen-centric solutions.
  • For International Relations, use India's interests, regional context and global developments.

📚 Major GS-II Areas

🏛️ Constitution

Constitutional provisions, amendments, rights and institutions.

⚖️ Polity

Parliament, Judiciary, Executive, federalism and constitutional bodies.

🧑‍💼 Governance

Transparency, accountability, e-governance and citizen participation.

🤝 Social Justice

Welfare schemes, vulnerable groups, health, education and inclusion.

🌍 International Relations

India's relations, neighbourhood, global institutions and diplomacy.
