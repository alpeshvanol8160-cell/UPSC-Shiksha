UPSC Daily Current Affairs – Prelims + Mains

GS-II POLITY

🏛️ CPGRAMS Monthly Report

Department of Administrative Reforms and Public Grievances (DARPG) released the 51st Monthly Report of CPGRAMS for Central Ministries and Departments for July 2026.

  • CPGRAMS – Centralized Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System
  • Focus: grievance redressal and citizen-centric governance
  • Important for: Governance, e-Governance and Accountability
GS-III ECONOMY

📊 Q1 FY 2026-27 GDP Estimates

MoSPI released the Quarterly Estimates of Gross Domestic Product for the first quarter (April–June) of financial year 2026-27.

  • Period covered: April–June 2026
  • Released by: Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation
  • Important concepts: GDP, GVA, economic growth and national accounts

UPSC Focus: Difference between GDP and GVA, real vs nominal GDP and methods of measuring national income.

GS-III INDUSTRY

🏭 Index of Industrial Production – July 2026

India’s Index of Industrial Production (IIP) recorded growth of 6.7% in July 2026.

  • IIP is an important indicator of industrial activity.
  • Major sectors covered include mining, manufacturing and electricity.
  • Base year and sectoral weights are important for UPSC.
GS-III STATISTICS

📈 Services Production – Experimental Index

MoSPI released the Sub-Sectoral Trial Index of Services Production for June 2026 with base year 2024–25.

The development is relevant for understanding measurement of activity in India’s services sector.

GS-II INTERNATIONAL RELATIONS

🇮🇳🇩🇪 Indo-German Environment Cooperation

The 4th Indo-German Environment Forum was scheduled to be held in New Delhi on 1 September 2026.

  • Theme: “Together For More Resilience”
  • Focus: environmental policy and climate cooperation
  • India–Germany bilateral environmental cooperation is an important area.
GS-III ENVIRONMENT

🌱 Indo-German Environment Forum

The forum focuses on cooperation between India and Germany on environment and climate-related issues.

UPSC Focus: Climate change, environmental governance, international environmental cooperation and sustainable development.

GS-III SCIENCE & TECHNOLOGY

⚛️ C-DOT Unveils Indigenous Quantum Products

Centre for Development of Telematics (C-DOT) unveiled 14 indigenous quantum products aimed at strengthening security of India’s communication networks.

  • C-DOT works under the Department of Telecommunications.
  • Quantum technologies have applications in secure communications.
  • Important areas: quantum communication, cybersecurity and encryption.
GS-II GS-III

📱 Sanchar Saathi – Lost Mobile Handsets

Sanchar Saathi recorded recovery of more than 75,000 lost and stolen mobile handsets in a single month, according to the Ministry of Communications.

UPSC Focus: Digital governance, telecom security, mobile-device identification and citizen-centric technology platforms.

GS-III SECURITY

🛡️ Communication Network Security

Indigenous quantum products developed by C-DOT are relevant to the broader objective of improving the security and resilience of communication networks.

Prelims Focus: C-DOT, Department of Telecommunications and quantum communication.

GS-II RURAL DEVELOPMENT

💧 Jal Jeevan Mission 2.0

The Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation urged States and Union Territories to facilitate student participation from local academic institutions under Jal Jeevan Mission 2.0.

  • Focus: rural drinking water supply
  • Important for: governance, public service delivery and rural development
  • UPSC linkage: water security and cooperative federalism
GS-III BLUE ECONOMY

🐟 Mission Rangeen Machhli 2031

During a Fisheries Outreach Programme at Agatti, Lakshadweep, the Strategic Action Plan “Mission Rangeen Machhli 2031” was released for ornamental fisheries development.

  • Focus: ornamental fisheries
  • Related to: Blue Economy
  • Important species/resources discussed include tuna and seaweed.
GS-III CRITICAL MINERALS

⛏️ Critical Minerals & Chhattisgarh

Chhattisgarh highlighted its role in India’s critical mineral ecosystem and mineral development.

UPSC Focus: Critical minerals, mineral security, industrial development and supply-chain resilience.
