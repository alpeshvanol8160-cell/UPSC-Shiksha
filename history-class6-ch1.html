1. 📖 What Is History?

History is the systematic study of the human past. It helps us understand how people lived, what they created, how societies changed and how events in the past shaped the world around us.

The past cannot be observed directly. Therefore, historians reconstruct it using evidence. Different types of evidence give different pieces of information, and historians compare these pieces before reaching conclusions.

⭐ Core Concept: History is not simply a list of dates and kings. It is an attempt to understand the lives, activities, ideas and changes of people in the past through available evidence.

2. ⏳ How Do We Measure Historical Time?

Historical events happened at different points in time. To arrange them correctly, historians use systems of dating and chronological order.

Years, Decades, Centuries and Millennia

📅 Year A single year is a basic unit for recording dates.
🔟 Decade A period of 10 years.
💯 Century A period of 100 years.
🌎 Millennium A period of 1,000 years.
Example: 2001 से 2100 तक का period 21st century है। इसी तरह 1901 से 2000 तक 20th century थी।

BCE and CE

Modern historical writing commonly uses BCE (Before Common Era) and CE (Common Era).

BCE The years before the beginning of the Common Era.
CE The years of the Common Era.
⚠️ Important: BCE dates move toward 1 as time approaches the beginning of the Common Era. For example, 500 BCE is earlier than 200 BCE.

5. 🔎 Who Studies the Past?

Understanding the past is a multidisciplinary activity. Different experts study different kinds of evidence.

🌍
Geologists Study the physical features and geological history of the Earth.
🦴
Palaeontologists Study fossils and remains of ancient plants and animals.
👥
Anthropologists Study humans, societies, cultures and human development.
🏺
Archaeologists Study material remains left behind by people and investigate archaeological sites.
UPSC Foundation: History often requires evidence from several disciplines. This is why archaeological, environmental and cultural evidence can complement written sources.

6. 🏺 Sources of History

Historical sources are the pieces of evidence from which we learn about the past. No single source always gives the complete picture.

🏺
Archaeological Sources Tools, pottery, beads, buildings, burials, ornaments, sculptures, coins and other material remains.
📜
Literary Sources Manuscripts, historical writings, poems, plays, religious literature, chronicles and other written material.
🗿
Inscriptions Writing engraved or recorded on materials such as stone or metal can provide valuable historical evidence.
🪙
Coins Coins can provide clues about rulers, symbols, economy, political authority and trade.
🏛️
Monuments and Structures Buildings, temples, forts, settlements and other structures can reveal technology, art, architecture and social organisation.
🗣️
Oral Traditions Stories, memories, songs and traditions transmitted orally can preserve information about communities and their past.

8. 🏺 Archaeology and Excavation

Archaeologists investigate places where remains of past human activity may survive. Excavation can reveal layers containing objects and structures from different periods.

What Can Archaeologists Find?

Tools Can provide clues about technology and activities.
Pottery Can reveal manufacturing techniques, food practices and exchange.
Buildings Can show settlement patterns and construction techniques.
Burials Can provide clues about customs and social practices.
Coins Can help study political and economic history.
Plant and Animal Remains Can provide information about diet, environment and subsistence.
Example: यदि excavation में pottery, animal bones और plant remains मिलते हैं, तो इन्हें साथ देखकर researchers उस community के food habits और daily life के बारे में questions पूछ सकते हैं।

9. 📜 Written and Literary Evidence

Written material can preserve ideas, events, traditions and information about societies. However, written sources also need to be studied critically.

Source Possible Information Careful Reading Needed
Manuscripts Ideas, stories, knowledge and traditions Who wrote it? When? For whom?
Inscriptions Orders, donations, achievements and public messages Purpose and historical context
Chronicles Events and accounts of rulers or societies Author and intended audience
Poems and Stories Cultural ideas and social memory Literary form and historical context
Important: A written source should not automatically be treated as a completely objective record. Historians consider its context, purpose and perspective.

11. 👣 Early Humans and the Past

The study of early human life depends heavily on archaeological, environmental and biological evidence because written records did not exist for many very early periods.

Hunter-Gatherer Life

Early human communities often depended on hunting, gathering, fishing and collecting available resources. Their movements could be connected to water, food availability, climate and seasonal changes.
