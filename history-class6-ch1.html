📖 Chapter Overview

History helps us understand how people lived, worked, travelled, fought, traded and organised their societies in the past.

Historians reconstruct the past using different kinds of evidence. These include manuscripts, inscriptions, coins, tools, buildings, pottery, bones and other archaeological remains.

🇬🇧 English Explanation

1. What is History?

History is the study of the human past. It helps us understand changes in society, economy, culture, technology and political life.

2. Where did early people live?

Early humans lived in different environments such as forests, grasslands, river valleys, mountains and coastal regions. Availability of water, food and other resources influenced human settlements and movement.

3. How do we know about the past?

Historians and archaeologists use different sources to reconstruct the past. Written sources include manuscripts and inscriptions. Archaeological sources include tools, pottery, coins, buildings, bones and other remains.

4. Manuscripts

Manuscripts were written by hand. They could be written on materials such as palm leaves, birch bark and other surfaces.

5. Inscriptions

Inscriptions are writings engraved or carved on hard surfaces such as stone or metal. They can provide information about rulers, events, donations and social life.

6. Archaeological Sources

Archaeologists study material remains from the past. Objects such as tools, pottery, ornaments, coins and structures can provide evidence about earlier societies.

7. Dates in History

Dates help historians arrange events in chronological order. BC/BCE and AD/CE are systems used to refer to historical time.

इतिहास क्या है?

इतिहास मानव अतीत का अध्ययन है। इससे हमें यह समझने में सहायता मिलती है कि अलग-अलग समय में लोग कैसे रहते थे, क्या खाते थे, किस प्रकार काम करते थे और समाज में क्या परिवर्तन हुए।

अतीत के बारे में जानकारी कैसे मिलती है?

इतिहासकार और पुरातत्वविद विभिन्न स्रोतों का अध्ययन करते हैं। पांडुलिपियाँ, अभिलेख, सिक्के, औजार, मिट्टी के बर्तन, इमारतें और मानव तथा पशु अवशेष महत्वपूर्ण स्रोत हैं।

🟢 ગુજરાતી માં સમજીએ

ઇતિહાસ શું છે?

ઇતિહાસ માનવ ભૂતકાળનો અભ્યાસ છે. તે આપણને ભૂતકાળના લોકોનું જીવન, તેમની પ્રવૃત્તિઓ અને સમાજમાં થયેલા ફેરફારો સમજવામાં મદદ કરે છે.

ભૂતકાળ વિશે માહિતી કેવી રીતે મળે છે?

ઇતિહાસકારો અને પુરાતત્વવિદો વિવિધ પુરાવાઓનો અભ્યાસ કરે છે. હસ્તપ્રતો, શિલાલેખો, સિક્કા, સાધનો, માટીના વાસણો અને ઇમારતો મહત્વપૂર્ણ સ્ત્રોતો છે.

🎯 UPSC Important Points

  • History is the study of the human past.
  • Historians use different sources to reconstruct the past.
  • Manuscripts are handwritten sources.
  • Inscriptions are writings engraved on hard surfaces.
  • Archaeology studies material remains.
  • Tools and pottery provide information about past societies.
  • Coins can provide information about economy and rulers.
