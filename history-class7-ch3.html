📖 1. Detailed English Explanation

Why Did Delhi Become Important?

Between the 12th and 15th centuries, Delhi developed into an important political centre of northern India. Its importance increased because rulers who controlled Delhi could exercise authority over large territories and important routes.

Key Idea: Delhi was not always the most important city. Its political importance grew gradually as different dynasties established their capitals and expanded their territories.

From Regional Centre to Imperial Capital

The early history of Delhi included the rule of the Tomaras and later the Chauhans. These rulers strengthened the political importance of Delhi and its surrounding region.

Later, Turkish rulers established their authority in Delhi and created what historians call the Delhi Sultanate.

🏰 2. The Rise of Delhi

Tomaras

The Tomaras were among the early Rajput dynasties associated with Delhi. Ananga Pala is an important ruler mentioned in the historical tradition of early Delhi.

The Tomaras contributed to the development of Delhi as a political and strategic centre.

Chauhans

The Chauhans later became powerful in the region. Prithviraj Chauhan was one of the best-known rulers of this dynasty.

UPSC Link: When studying early medieval India, remember that political power was often divided among several regional dynasties rather than being controlled by one empire.

👑 3. Delhi Sultanate

The Delhi Sultanate refers to a series of dynasties that ruled from Delhi between the early 13th century and the early 16th century. This chapter focuses on the period from the 12th to the 15th century.

Early Turkish Rulers

After the establishment of Turkish rule in Delhi, rulers such as Qutbuddin Aybak, Iltutmish, Raziyya and Balban became important figures.

Qutbuddin Aybak

Qutbuddin Aybak was among the early Turkish rulers of Delhi. His rule marked an important phase in the establishment of the Sultanate.

Iltutmish

Shamsuddin Iltutmish consolidated the Sultanate and strengthened Delhi's position as the centre of political authority.

Raziyya

Raziyya was the daughter of Iltutmish and became ruler of Delhi. Her reign is significant because she was one of the few women to occupy the position of Sultan in the Delhi Sultanate.

Balban

Ghiyasuddin Balban followed a strong approach to kingship and emphasised the authority and dignity of the Sultan.

🏛️ 6. How Did Sultans Govern?

The Sultanate rulers needed an organised administrative system because they controlled large territories containing different populations, local chiefs and regional elites.

Military Power

Military strength was central to the expansion and protection of the Sultanate. Cavalry and horses were particularly important.

Control of Territories

A Sultan could not personally control every village or region. Officials, commanders and local authorities therefore played important roles in administration.

Revenue

Revenue from agriculture was an important source of state income. Control over productive agricultural regions helped rulers maintain their armies and administration.

⚔️ Army

Protected territories and helped rulers expand their authority.

💰 Revenue

Agricultural production was an important source of state income.

🏛️ Administration

Officials and local authorities helped manage large territories.

⚔️ 7. Khalji Dynasty

Alauddin Khalji

Alauddin Khalji was one of the most powerful rulers of the Delhi Sultanate. He expanded the Sultanate into several regions and developed administrative and military measures to support his campaigns.

His period is particularly important for understanding the relationship between military expansion, revenue and the maintenance of a large army.

UPSC Concept: Expansion of territory creates administrative challenges. A ruler needs resources, military organisation and systems for collecting revenue.

🏛️ 8. Tughluq Dynasty

Muhammad Tughluq

Muhammad Tughluq is remembered for several ambitious administrative experiments and policies. His reign shows how difficult it could be to manage a large and diverse empire.

Firuz Shah Tughluq

Firuz Shah Tughluq is associated with public works, irrigation projects and administrative activities.

Historical Method: A policy should be studied through multiple sources and its historical context rather than through a single isolated statement.

📜 10. Historical Sources

Historians reconstruct the history of the Delhi Sultanate using different kinds of evidence.

📜 Inscriptions

Inscriptions can provide information about rulers, donations, buildings and political claims.

🪙 Coins

Coins can provide evidence about rulers, titles, economy and political authority.

📚 Texts

Historical writings can provide information about administration, society and political events, while also requiring critical analysis.

Important: Historians compare different sources because every source has its own purpose, limitations and point of view.

🇮🇳 13. विस्तृत हिन्दी व्याख्या

दिल्ली का महत्व कैसे बढ़ा?

12वीं से 15वीं शताब्दी के बीच दिल्ली एक महत्वपूर्ण राजनीतिक केन्द्र बनकर उभरी। अलग-अलग शासकों ने दिल्ली को अपनी सत्ता के केन्द्र के रूप में विकसित किया।

तोमर और चौहान

दिल्ली के प्रारम्भिक इतिहास में तोमर शासकों का महत्वपूर्ण स्थान था। अनंगपाल का नाम प्रारम्भिक दिल्ली के इतिहास से जुड़ा हुआ है।

इसके बाद चौहान शक्ति का प्रभाव बढ़ा। पृथ्वीराज चौहान इस वंश के प्रमुख शासकों में से एक थे।

मुख्य बात: दिल्ली का राजनीतिक महत्व धीरे-धीरे बढ़ा और बाद में यह दिल्ली सल्तनत का प्रमुख केन्द्र बनी।

दिल्ली सल्तनत

दिल्ली सल्तनत एक ही वंश का शासन नहीं था। अलग-अलग राजवंशों ने अलग-अलग समय में दिल्ली से शासन किया।

प्रारम्भिक तुर्क शासक

कुतुबुद्दीन ऐबक, इल्तुतमिश, रज़िया और बलबन प्रारम्भिक तुर्क शासकों में प्रमुख थे।

रज़िया

रज़िया इल्तुतमिश की पुत्री थीं और दिल्ली की सुल्तान बनीं। उनका शासन मध्यकालीन भारतीय इतिहास में महिलाओं की राजनीतिक भूमिका को समझने के लिए महत्वपूर्ण है।

खिलजी वंश

अलाउद्दीन खिलजी ने सल्तनत की शक्ति का विस्तार किया। उनके शासन को सैन्य विस्तार, प्रशासन और राजस्व व्यवस्था के संदर्भ में पढ़ना महत्वपूर्ण है।

तुगलक वंश

मुहम्मद तुगलक की कई महत्वाकांक्षी नीतियां इतिहास में प्रसिद्ध हैं। फिरोज शाह तुगलक का संबंध सिंचाई और सार्वजनिक निर्माण जैसे कार्यों से जोड़ा जाता है।

राजस्व और सेना

बड़े राज्य को चलाने के लिए शासकों को सेना, अधिकारियों और राजस्व व्यवस्था की आवश्यकता थी। कृषि से मिलने वाला राजस्व राज्य की आय का महत्वपूर्ण स्रोत था।

इतिहास के स्रोत

इस काल के इतिहास को समझने के लिए सिक्के, अभिलेख और लिखित ग्रंथ महत्वपूर्ण स्रोत हैं।

UPSC दृष्टिकोण: किसी ऐतिहासिक घटना को समझते समय केवल एक स्रोत पर निर्भर नहीं करना चाहिए। अलग-अलग स्रोतों की तुलना करनी चाहिए।
