🌿 1. Ecology & Ecosystem

Ecology deals with interactions among organisms and between organisms and their physical environment.

Aspects / Details

Aspect Details
Ecology Study of interactions between organisms and environment.
Ecosystem Functional unit consisting of biotic and abiotic components.
Biotic Components Plants, animals, microorganisms.
Abiotic Components Water, air, soil, temperature, sunlight and minerals.
Producers Organisms that produce food, mainly green plants.
Consumers Organisms depending directly or indirectly on producers.
Decomposers Bacteria and fungi that break down organic matter.

🟢 Prelims Focus

  • Food chain
  • Ecological pyramid
  • Producers and consumers
  • Decomposers

🟠 Mains Focus

  • Ecosystem stability
  • Human impact
  • Conservation
  • Climate stress

🔴 Important

Energy flow in an ecosystem is generally unidirectional.

🐾 2. Biodiversity

Biodiversity refers to the variety of life at genetic, species and ecosystem levels.

🧬 Genetic Diversity
🐅 Species Diversity
🌳 Ecosystem Diversity
🌍 Global Biodiversity

Major Threats

Threat Example / Effect
Habitat Loss Deforestation, urbanisation and infrastructure development.
Invasive Species Non-native species may disturb native ecosystems.
Pollution Air, water, soil and plastic pollution.
Climate Change Changes habitats, migration and species distribution.
Overexploitation Unsustainable hunting, fishing and resource extraction.

🔴 UPSC Important

Study biodiversity together with hotspots, endemic species, IUCN categories, protected areas and conservation strategies.

🌡️ 3. Climate Change & Global Warming

Concept Explanation
Greenhouse Effect Atmospheric gases absorb and re-radiate heat.
Global Warming Long-term rise in Earth's average temperature.
Climate Change Long-term changes in temperature, rainfall and other climate patterns.
Carbon Cycle Movement of carbon among atmosphere, organisms, oceans and geological systems.
Mitigation Reducing greenhouse-gas emissions or enhancing carbon sinks.
Adaptation Adjusting systems and communities to climate impacts.

🟢 Prelims

  • Greenhouse gases
  • Carbon sinks
  • Climate terminology
  • International agreements

🟠 Mains

  • Climate justice
  • India's challenges
  • Adaptation
  • Mitigation

💡 Remember

Mitigation addresses the causes; adaptation addresses the impacts.

🐅 5. Protected Areas & Wildlife Conservation

Category Basic Idea
National Park Protected area with stronger restrictions on human activities.
Wildlife Sanctuary Area established primarily for protection of wildlife and habitat.
Biosphere Reserve Large conservation landscape combining biodiversity protection with sustainable-use objectives.
Tiger Reserve Landscape designated for tiger conservation.
Ramsar Site Wetland recognised under the Ramsar Convention framework.

🔴 UPSC Important

For protected areas, prepare location, ecosystem type, important species, special features and associated conservation programmes.

⚖️ 6. Environmental Laws & Institutions

Law / Institution UPSC Relevance
Wildlife Protection Framework Wildlife and protected-area conservation.
Forest Conservation Framework Regulation of diversion of forest land.
Environment Protection Framework Broad legal framework for environmental protection.
Biological Diversity Framework Conservation and sustainable use of biological resources.
Pollution Control Institutions Monitoring and regulation of pollution.

🟢 Prelims Focus

Focus on objectives, institutions, protected-area categories, environmental clearances and important terminology.

♻️ 8. Sustainable Development

Sustainable development seeks to balance economic development, social needs and environmental protection.

Dimension Focus
Economic Growth, employment and resource efficiency.
Social Equity, health, education and livelihoods.
Environmental Conservation, pollution reduction and climate resilience.

🟠 Mains Approach

Environment answers become stronger when economic, social and ecological dimensions are connected instead of treating conservation in isolation.

Concept Meaning
Food Chain Linear transfer of food and energy between organisms.
Food Web Interconnected food chains within an ecosystem.

🏗️ 12. Environmental Impact Assessment

Environmental Impact Assessment is used to assess potential environmental effects of proposed projects before decisions are made.

1️⃣ Screening

Determines whether detailed assessment is required.

2️⃣ Assessment

Studies likely environmental impacts.

3️⃣ Decision

Environmental considerations are incorporated into project decisions.
