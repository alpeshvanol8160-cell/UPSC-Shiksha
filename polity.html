📚 Indian Polity for UPSC

Indian Polity is an important area for UPSC Prelims and Mains. Study the Constitution, Fundamental Rights, Parliament, Executive, Judiciary, Federalism, Constitutional Bodies and Governance systematically.

🇮🇳 1. Indian Constitution

The Constitution of India provides the framework for governance, institutions, powers and rights in India.

Important Areas

  • Historical Background
  • Constituent Assembly
  • Making of the Constitution
  • Salient Features
  • Preamble
  • Parts and Schedules

HIGH IMPORTANCE

⚖️ 2. Fundamental Rights

Fundamental Rights are contained in Part III of the Constitution.

Major Rights

  • Right to Equality
  • Right to Freedom
  • Right against Exploitation
  • Right to Freedom of Religion
  • Cultural and Educational Rights
  • Right to Constitutional Remedies

Important Concepts

  • Writs
  • Reasonable Restrictions
  • Article 32
  • Article 226
  • Judicial Review
PRELIMS

🫡 3. Fundamental Duties

Fundamental Duties are contained in Article 51A under Part IVA.

  • Respect the Constitution and national symbols
  • Protect sovereignty and integrity of India
  • Promote harmony
  • Protect the environment
  • Develop scientific temper
  • Safeguard public property
  • Provide educational opportunities to children as specified by the Constitution
