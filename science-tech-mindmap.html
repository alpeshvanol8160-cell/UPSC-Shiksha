🚀 1. Space Technology

Space technology covers satellites, launch systems, remote sensing, navigation, communication and space exploration.

Aspects / Details

Aspect Details
Launch Vehicles Systems used to place spacecraft and satellites into orbit.
Satellites Used for communication, weather, navigation, Earth observation and science.
Remote Sensing Collection of information about Earth's surface using sensors.
Navigation Satellite-based positioning and navigation services.
Space Exploration Scientific missions to study the Moon, planets and other celestial objects.

🟢 Prelims Focus

  • Satellite types
  • Orbits
  • Launch vehicles
  • Remote sensing
  • Navigation systems

🟠 Mains Focus

  • Space applications
  • Disaster management
  • Agriculture
  • Communication
  • Strategic importance

🔴 Important

Connect space technology with agriculture, weather forecasting, communication and disaster management.

🧬 2. Biotechnology

Biotechnology uses biological systems, organisms or components for useful applications in medicine, agriculture, industry and environment.

🧬 Genetic Engineering
🧪 Gene Editing
🌾 Agricultural Biotech
💉 Medical Biotech
Topic UPSC Relevance
Genetic Engineering Modification of genetic material for desired characteristics.
Gene Editing Targeted changes to genetic material.
GM Crops Crops developed using genetic modification techniques.
Vaccines Biotechnology contributes to vaccine development and production.
Biofuels Biological resources can be used to produce energy.

☢️ 3. Nuclear Technology

Concept Explanation
Nuclear Fission Splitting of a heavy atomic nucleus and release of energy.
Nuclear Fusion Combining light nuclei under suitable conditions to release energy.
Nuclear Power Uses nuclear reactions as a source of energy.
Radioisotopes Used in medicine, industry and scientific applications.
Nuclear Safety Focuses on safe operation, radiation protection and waste management.

🟢 Prelims

Understand fission, fusion, radiation, reactor basics and peaceful applications of nuclear technology.

🤖 4. Artificial Intelligence, Robotics & Automation

Technology Basic Idea
Artificial Intelligence Systems designed to perform tasks involving capabilities associated with human intelligence.
Machine Learning Algorithms that learn patterns from data.
Deep Learning Machine-learning methods based on multi-layer neural networks.
Robotics Design and use of programmable machines capable of performing tasks.
Generative AI AI systems capable of generating content such as text, images or code.

🟢 Prelims

  • AI terminology
  • Machine learning
  • Robotics
  • Generative AI

🟠 Mains

  • AI in governance
  • Employment
  • Privacy
  • Ethics
  • Digital divide

💡 Answer Tip

For AI answers, discuss both opportunities and risks such as bias, privacy and accountability.

🔬 7. Nanotechnology

Nanotechnology deals with materials and systems at extremely small scales, where materials may show useful properties different from their bulk form.

Field Applications
Medicine Drug delivery, diagnostics and medical materials.
Energy Advanced materials for batteries and energy technologies.
Agriculture Potential applications in inputs, sensing and delivery systems.
Environment Water treatment and pollution monitoring technologies.

💉 8. Health & Medical Technology

Technology Application
Vaccines Prevention of infectious diseases.
Gene Therapy Uses genetic material or genetic modification approaches to address disease.
Telemedicine Delivery of healthcare services using communication technology.
Medical Imaging Tools such as MRI, CT and ultrasound support diagnosis.
Wearable Technology Devices used to monitor health-related parameters.
