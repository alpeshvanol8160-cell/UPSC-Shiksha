📚 Geography for UPSC

Geography is an important part of UPSC preparation. It connects physical processes, human activities, resources, climate, agriculture, industries and regional development.

🎯 Preparation Tip: Study Geography with maps, diagrams, concepts, current affairs and UPSC Previous Year Questions.

🌱 11. Indian Soils

Soil Major Features
Alluvial Soil Extensive in northern plains and river valleys
Black Soil Associated particularly with the Deccan region
Red Soil Common in parts of peninsular India
Laterite Soil Develops under high rainfall and leaching conditions
Desert Soil Found mainly in arid and semi-arid regions
Mountain Soil Occurs in Himalayan and other mountainous areas

🎯 13. How to Prepare Geography

Step 1 — Understand Concepts

Learn basic physical and human geography before moving to advanced topics.

Step 2 — Practice Maps

Regularly practice India and World maps.

Step 3 — Connect Current Affairs

Connect geographical concepts with climate events, disasters, resources, agriculture and international developments.

Step 4 — Solve PYQs

Practice UPSC previous year questions and identify repeatedly tested concepts.

📚 Best Approach: Concepts + Maps + Current Affairs + PYQs + Regular Revision.
