🇬🇧 English Explanation

1. What was Vijayanagara?

Vijayanagara literally means “City of Victory.” It was the name of both a powerful empire and its imperial capital. The empire emerged in southern India during the fourteenth century and became one of the important political powers of the medieval Deccan and South India.

UPSC Concept: Vijayanagara should be studied not only as a political empire but also as an example of urban planning, temple architecture, irrigation, trade and cultural interaction.

2. The Location of Vijayanagara

The capital developed in the region around the Tungabhadra River. The surrounding landscape contained rocky hills, river valleys and fertile areas. The natural geography helped the city in defence as well as agriculture and water management.

🌊 Tungabhadra

The river was important for water supply, agriculture and the development of the capital.

⛰️ Rocky Landscape

Hills and boulders provided natural defensive advantages around the city.

🌾 Fertile Areas

Agricultural production supported the large population and political centre.

3. Hampi — The Historic Landscape

The ruins of Vijayanagara are today associated with Hampi in Karnataka. After the destruction and decline of the imperial capital, the settlement became a major archaeological landscape.

The historical reconstruction of Hampi uses several types of evidence: monuments, inscriptions, archaeological remains, local traditions and accounts of foreign travellers.

Remember: History is reconstructed by comparing different sources rather than depending on a single source.

4. Discovery and Study of Hampi

The ruins attracted scholarly attention during the colonial period. Surveying, photography, inscriptional studies and archaeological work helped historians understand the former capital.

Information from local traditions was also compared with written records and accounts of travellers.

5. Foundation and Expansion

Vijayanagara was founded in the fourteenth century. Its rulers gradually expanded their political influence across large parts of southern India.

The empire had to compete with other regional powers for territory, resources, trade routes and political influence.

6. Major Rulers and Dynasties

The Vijayanagara Empire was ruled by different dynasties. Among them, the Sangama, Saluva, Tuluva and Aravidu dynasties are important in the historical sequence.

14th Century

Foundation and consolidation of Vijayanagara power.

15th Century

Expansion of the empire and development of the capital.

16th Century

Vijayanagara reached a major phase of political and cultural prominence.

1565

The capital suffered major destruction following the Battle of Talikota.

7. Krishnadeva Raya

Krishnadeva Raya of the Tuluva dynasty was one of the best-known rulers associated with Vijayanagara's power and prosperity.

His reign is remembered for military campaigns, political expansion, support for temples and cultural activities.

UPSC Point: Krishnadeva Raya is important for understanding the relationship between political authority, military expansion, temple patronage and cultural production.

8. Fortifications and City Planning

Vijayanagara had extensive fortifications. Walls, gateways and natural landscape features contributed to the defence of the city.

The urban area contained different zones including residential areas, sacred centres, royal spaces, markets and agricultural zones.

🧱 Fortifications

Walls and gateways strengthened defence.

🏛️ Royal Centre

Contained important administrative and ceremonial structures.

🛕 Sacred Centre

Contained major temples and religious institutions.

🛍️ Markets

Supported trade and exchange of goods.

9. Water Management

Water management was essential because the capital had a large population and required water for agriculture, domestic use and other activities.

Tanks, canals, reservoirs and channels helped distribute water. The Tungabhadra and surrounding landscape played an important role in this system.

10. Agriculture and Irrigation

Agricultural production was necessary to sustain the population, army and urban economy. Irrigation helped increase the availability and reliability of cultivated land.

The connection between agriculture and political power is important: agricultural surplus could support taxation, armies, temples and urban centres.

11. Temples and Architecture

Temples were important religious as well as social and economic institutions.

Large temple complexes required architects, sculptors, builders, craft workers and financial resources. They also became important centres of cultural activity.

🛕 Virupaksha Temple

A major temple associated with the sacred landscape of Hampi.

🏛️ Vitthala Temple

Known for its monumental architecture and famous stone structures.

🪨 Stone Architecture

Massive stone construction reflects skilled craftsmanship.

12. Markets and Trade

Markets were an important part of the urban economy. Goods from different regions could reach the city through trade networks.

The capital's political importance, wealthy elites and religious centres created demand for food, luxury goods, craft products and other items.

13. Foreign Travellers

Foreign travellers left descriptions of Vijayanagara. Their accounts provide useful information about the city, markets, royal ceremonies, buildings and social life.

However, historians do not accept every traveller's statement without comparison. Traveller accounts are evaluated alongside archaeological and inscriptional evidence.

Historical Method: A traveller's account is a source of evidence, not automatically a complete or unbiased description of the entire society.

14. Social and Economic Life

The large capital contained rulers, soldiers, administrators, merchants, craft workers, farmers, religious specialists and many other groups.

The city therefore represented a complex relationship between political authority, agriculture, trade, religion and urban life.

🇮🇳 हिंदी में विस्तृत अध्ययन

1. विजयनगर क्या था?

विजयनगर का अर्थ सामान्य रूप से “विजय का नगर” माना जाता है। यह नाम एक साम्राज्य और उसकी राजधानी दोनों के लिए प्रयोग किया जाता था। चौदहवीं शताब्दी में इसका उदय हुआ और दक्षिण भारत की राजनीति में इसका महत्वपूर्ण स्थान बना।

2. हम्पी और विजयनगर

आज विजयनगर की राजधानी के अवशेष कर्नाटक के हम्पी क्षेत्र में दिखाई देते हैं। यह क्षेत्र तुंगभद्रा नदी और पहाड़ी भू-दृश्य के आसपास विकसित हुआ था।

मुख्य बात: हम्पी के इतिहास को समझने के लिए पुरातत्व, स्थापत्य, अभिलेख, स्थानीय परंपराओं और विदेशी यात्रियों के विवरणों को साथ पढ़ना जरूरी है।

3. प्राकृतिक स्थिति

तुंगभद्रा नदी ने जल की उपलब्धता में सहायता की। आसपास की चट्टानी पहाड़ियाँ प्राकृतिक सुरक्षा प्रदान करती थीं। कृषि के लिए सिंचाई व्यवस्था विकसित की गई।

4. साम्राज्य का विस्तार

विजयनगर के शासकों ने दक्षिण भारत के बड़े क्षेत्रों पर अपना राजनीतिक प्रभाव स्थापित किया। अलग-अलग समय में साम्राज्य का क्षेत्र और राजनीतिक शक्ति बदलती रही।

5. कृष्णदेव राय

कृष्णदेव राय तुलुव वंश के प्रमुख शासकों में से थे। उनके शासनकाल को विजयनगर की राजनीतिक और सांस्कृतिक शक्ति के महत्वपूर्ण चरण के रूप में देखा जाता है।

उनके समय में सैन्य अभियानों, राजनीतिक विस्तार, मंदिरों के संरक्षण और सांस्कृतिक गतिविधियों को महत्व मिला।

6. किलेबंदी और नगर योजना

विजयनगर में विशाल किलेबंदी, प्रवेश द्वार और प्राकृतिक सुरक्षा व्यवस्था का उपयोग किया गया। नगर में राजकीय क्षेत्र, धार्मिक क्षेत्र, बाजार और अन्य गतिविधियों के लिए अलग-अलग स्थान थे।

7. जल प्रबंधन

बड़ी आबादी और कृषि को पानी उपलब्ध कराने के लिए जलाशयों, नहरों, तालाबों और अन्य जल संरचनाओं का उपयोग किया गया।

8. मंदिर और स्थापत्य

मंदिर केवल धार्मिक स्थान नहीं थे। वे सामाजिक, आर्थिक और सांस्कृतिक गतिविधियों के महत्वपूर्ण केन्द्र भी हो सकते थे।

विरुपाक्ष मंदिर और विट्ठल मंदिर हम्पी के प्रसिद्ध स्थापत्य उदाहरण हैं। इनमें पत्थर की उत्कृष्ट शिल्पकला दिखाई देती है।

9. बाजार और व्यापार

विजयनगर जैसे बड़े शहरी केन्द्र में विभिन्न प्रकार की वस्तुओं का व्यापार होता था। कृषि उत्पादन, शिल्प और व्यापार ने शहरी अर्थव्यवस्था को मजबूत किया।

10. विदेशी यात्री

विभिन्न विदेशी यात्रियों ने विजयनगर का वर्णन किया। उनके विवरणों से बाजार, राजदरबार, भवनों और सामाजिक जीवन के बारे में जानकारी मिलती है।

लेकिन इतिहासकार इन विवरणों को अन्य स्रोतों से मिलाकर देखते हैं।

12. 1565 और विजयनगर का पतन

1565 में तालिकोटा के युद्ध के बाद विजयनगर की राजधानी को भारी विनाश का सामना करना पड़ा। इसके बाद राजधानी की पूर्व राजनीतिक महत्ता कमजोर हुई।

📌 UPSC Important Points

  • Vijayanagara was both an empire and its capital city.
  • Hampi is the major archaeological site associated with Vijayanagara.
  • The Tungabhadra River was important for the region.
  • Natural hills and fortifications contributed to defence.
  • Krishnadeva Raya was a major ruler of the Tuluva dynasty.
  • Water management was essential for agriculture and urban life.
  • Temples were important religious, social and economic institutions.
  • Markets connected the capital with wider economic networks.
  • Foreign travellers provide valuable but limited perspectives.
  • Inscriptions, monuments, coins and archaeology help reconstruct history.
  • 1565 is associated with the major destruction of the capital after the Battle of Talikota.
