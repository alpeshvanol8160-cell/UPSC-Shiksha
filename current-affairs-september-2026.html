✍️ Mains Answer Writing

Question 1 — GS-II

"Urban housing programmes can contribute to inclusive urban development." Discuss the challenges and suggest measures for improving housing delivery.

Question 2 — GS-III

"Semiconductor manufacturing is increasingly linked with economic security and strategic autonomy." Discuss India's opportunities and challenges.

Question 3 — GS-III

Explain the significance of geothermal energy in India's transition towards a cleaner and diversified energy mix.

Question 4 — GS-III

Discuss the opportunities and environmental challenges associated with India's Blue Economy.
