UPSC CSE SYLLABUS

Complete UPSC Syllabus

Prelims, CSAT and Mains syllabus — organized for easy preparation.

📚 General Studies Paper-I

  • Current Events of National and International Importance
  • History of India and Indian National Movement
  • Indian and World Geography
  • Indian Polity and Governance
  • Economic and Social Development
  • Environment, Ecology, Biodiversity and Climate Change
  • General Science
📚 View Full Prelims Syllabus

🧠 CSAT – General Studies Paper-II

  • Comprehension
  • Interpersonal Skills including Communication Skills
  • Logical Reasoning and Analytical Ability
  • Decision Making and Problem Solving
  • General Mental Ability
  • Basic Numeracy
  • Data Interpretation
🧠 View Full CSAT Syllabus

🗣️ Paper-A — Indian Language

One Indian language selected by the candidate from the languages included in the Eighth Schedule. This is a qualifying paper.

🇬🇧 Paper-B — English

English language paper testing comprehension, précis writing, usage and vocabulary, and short essays. This is a qualifying paper.

✍️ Paper-I — Essay

Candidates may be required to write essays on multiple topics.

📖 Paper-II — General Studies-I

  • Indian Heritage and Culture
  • History and Geography of the World
  • Society
📖 GS-I Practice

🏛️ Paper-III — General Studies-II

  • Governance
  • Constitution
  • Polity
  • Social Justice
  • International Relations
🏛️ GS-II Practice

💰 Paper-IV — General Studies-III

  • Technology
  • Economic Development
  • Biodiversity and Environment
  • Security and Disaster Management
💰 GS-III Practice

⚖️ Paper-V — General Studies-IV

  • Ethics
  • Integrity
  • Aptitude
  • Emotional Intelligence
  • Probity in Governance
⚖️ GS-IV Practice

🎓 Paper-VI & VII — Optional Subject

One optional subject with two papers. Candidates choose the optional subject according to UPSC rules.
