UPSC Civil Services Preliminary Examination

The Civil Services Preliminary Examination is the first stage of the UPSC Civil Services Examination. It consists of two objective-type papers: General Studies Paper I and General Studies Paper II (CSAT).

📚 GENERAL STUDIES PAPER – I

Objective Type • General Studies

1. 📰 Current Events of National and International Importance

Current affairs of national and international importance, including major events, government policies, important developments and issues affecting India and the world.

2. 🏛️ History of India and Indian National Movement

History of India with emphasis on the broad aspects of Indian national movement and the freedom struggle.

  • Ancient India
  • Medieval India
  • Modern India
  • Indian National Movement
  • Freedom Struggle

3. 🌍 Indian and World Geography

Physical, social and economic geography of India and the world.

  • Physical Geography
  • Indian Geography
  • World Geography
  • Climate and Weather
  • Natural Resources
  • Population and Human Geography
  • Economic Geography

4. ⚖️ Indian Polity and Governance

Constitution, political system, Panchayati Raj, public policy, rights issues and other aspects of governance.

  • Indian Constitution
  • Fundamental Rights
  • Fundamental Duties
  • Directive Principles of State Policy
  • Parliament
  • President and Vice-President
  • Prime Minister and Council of Ministers
  • Supreme Court and High Courts
  • Election System
  • Panchayati Raj
  • Governance and Public Policy

5. 💰 Economic and Social Development

Sustainable development, poverty, inclusion, demographics, social sector initiatives and other economic and social issues.

  • Indian Economy
  • Economic Growth and Development
  • Poverty and Unemployment
  • Inclusive Growth
  • Government Schemes
  • Demographics
  • Social Sector Initiatives
  • Sustainable Development

6. 🌱 Environmental Ecology, Biodiversity and Climate Change

General issues on environmental ecology, biodiversity and climate change that do not require subject specialisation.

  • Ecology and Ecosystems
  • Biodiversity
  • Environmental Pollution
  • Climate Change
  • Global Warming
  • Conservation
  • National Parks and Wildlife Sanctuaries
  • Environmental Conventions

7. 🔬 General Science

General understanding of science and technology and their applications in everyday life and society.

  • Physics – Basic Concepts
  • Chemistry – Basic Concepts
  • Biology
  • Space and Technology
  • Information Technology
  • Biotechnology
  • Defence Technology
  • Recent Scientific Developments

🧠 GENERAL STUDIES PAPER – II (CSAT)

Qualifying Paper • Objective Type

General Studies Paper II (CSAT) is a qualifying paper in the Preliminary Examination. Candidates are required to secure the minimum qualifying marks prescribed by UPSC.

No. CSAT Syllabus
1 Comprehension
2 Interpersonal skills including communication skills
3 Logical reasoning and analytical ability
4 Decision-making and problem-solving
5 General mental ability
6 Basic numeracy and data interpretation (Class X level)
7 Questions relating to English Language Comprehension skills (Class X level)

⚠️ Important Note

The syllabus presented on UPSC Shiksha is intended as a study-reference page. Candidates should always check the latest official UPSC examination notification for the syllabus applicable to their examination year.
