UPSC Civil Services Main Examination

The Civil Services Main Examination is the second stage of the UPSC Civil Services Examination. It consists of written papers designed to assess a candidate's intellectual abilities, understanding, analytical skills and ability to express ideas clearly.

📋 Mains Examination Structure

Written Examination • Descriptive Papers

📌 Mains Papers

  • Paper A – Indian Language (Qualifying)
  • Paper B – English (Qualifying)
  • Paper I – Essay
  • Paper II – General Studies I
  • Paper III – General Studies II
  • Paper IV – General Studies III
  • Paper V – General Studies IV
  • Paper VI – Optional Subject Paper I
  • Paper VII – Optional Subject Paper II

🗣️ QUALIFYING PAPERS

Paper A and Paper B

1. 🇮🇳 Paper A – Indian Language

One Indian language selected by the candidate from the languages included in the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution, subject to the conditions prescribed by UPSC.

  • Comprehension of given passages
  • Precis Writing
  • Usage and Vocabulary
  • Short Essay
  • Translation from English to the Indian language
  • Translation from the Indian language to English

2. 🇬🇧 Paper B – English

The English paper is qualifying in nature.

  • Comprehension of given passages
  • Precis Writing
  • Usage and Vocabulary
  • Short Essay

📝 PAPER I – ESSAY

Essay Paper

Essay

Candidates may be required to write essays on multiple topics. They are expected to keep closely to the subject of the essay, arrange their ideas in an orderly fashion and write concisely.

  • Social Issues
  • Political Issues
  • Economic Issues
  • Philosophical Topics
  • Science and Technology
  • Environment
  • International Relations
  • Current Affairs

📚 PAPER II – GENERAL STUDIES I

Indian Heritage, Culture, History, Geography and Society

1. Indian Heritage & Culture

  • Indian Art Forms
  • Literature
  • Architecture
  • Culture and Traditions

2. Modern Indian History

  • Events from the middle of the eighteenth century
  • Indian Freedom Struggle
  • Important personalities
  • Post-independence consolidation

3. World History

  • Industrial Revolution
  • World Wars
  • Nationalism
  • Colonization and Decolonization
  • Political philosophies

4. Indian Society

  • Indian Society and Diversity
  • Women and Women's Organizations
  • Population Issues
  • Poverty and Development
  • Urbanization
  • Social Empowerment

5. Geography

  • Physical Geography
  • Important Geophysical Phenomena
  • Earthquakes and Tsunamis
  • Volcanic Activity
  • Changes in water bodies and ice-caps
  • Important geographical features

🏛️ PAPER III – GENERAL STUDIES II

Governance, Constitution, Polity, Social Justice and International Relations

1. Indian Constitution & Polity

  • Constitution and its evolution
  • Federalism
  • Separation of powers
  • Parliament and State Legislatures
  • Executive and Judiciary
  • Constitutional bodies

2. Governance

  • Government policies
  • Transparency and accountability
  • E-Governance
  • Citizen Charters
  • Role of civil services

3. Social Justice

  • Welfare schemes
  • Issues related to health
  • Education
  • Poverty and hunger
  • Vulnerable sections

4. International Relations

  • India and neighbouring countries
  • Bilateral relations
  • International institutions
  • Global groupings
  • International issues

💰 PAPER IV – GENERAL STUDIES III

Technology, Economy, Environment, Security and Disaster Management

1. Indian Economy

  • Economic development
  • Inclusive growth
  • Government budgeting
  • Agriculture
  • Food processing
  • Infrastructure

2. Science & Technology

  • Science and technological developments
  • Space technology
  • Biotechnology
  • Information technology
  • Robotics and AI

3. Environment & Biodiversity

  • Environmental pollution
  • Conservation
  • Biodiversity
  • Climate change
  • Environmental impact assessment

4. Internal Security

  • Internal security challenges
  • Cyber security
  • Money laundering
  • Border security
  • Organized crime
  • Disaster management

⚖️ PAPER V – GENERAL STUDIES IV

Ethics, Integrity and Aptitude

1. Ethics & Human Interface

  • Essence of ethics
  • Determinants of ethical behaviour
  • Consequences of ethics
  • Human values
  • Role of family and society

2. Attitude

  • Content and structure of attitude
  • Influence on thought and behaviour
  • Moral and political attitudes
  • Social influence and persuasion

3. Emotional Intelligence

  • Concept of emotional intelligence
  • Application in administration
  • Understanding emotions

4. Integrity & Aptitude

  • Probity in governance
  • Transparency
  • Accountability
  • Ethical governance
  • Code of ethics
  • Case studies

🎓 PAPER VI & VII – OPTIONAL SUBJECT

Optional Subject Paper I and Paper II

Optional Subject

Candidates have to choose one optional subject. The optional subject consists of two papers.

  • Optional Paper I
  • Optional Paper II
  • Each paper covers the prescribed optional syllabus
