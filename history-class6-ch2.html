1. Chapter Overview

This chapter introduces us to some of the earliest human communities. These people lived thousands of years ago, long before the development of cities, kingdoms and written records.

They survived mainly by hunting animals, gathering plants, collecting fruits, roots, seeds and other naturally available resources.

⭐ UPSC Concept: The study of early humans helps us understand the development of technology, food habits, settlement patterns and the gradual transition from hunting-gathering to food production.
💡 Simple Example:
Imagine a group of people living in a forest without shops, farms or modern tools. They would have to depend directly on nature for food, water, shelter and materials.

2. Who Were Hunter-Gatherers?

English Explanation

Hunter-gatherers were communities that obtained food directly from their natural surroundings. They hunted animals and gathered edible plants, fruits, roots, seeds, eggs and other resources.

They generally did not produce food through large-scale farming. Because food resources could become scarce in one location, many groups moved from one place to another.

हिंदी में समझें

शिकारी-संग्रहकर्ता वे प्रारंभिक मानव समुदाय थे जो अपने आसपास के प्राकृतिक संसाधनों से भोजन प्राप्त करते थे। वे जानवरों का शिकार करते और फल, जड़ें, बीज, अंडे तथा अन्य खाद्य पदार्थ एकत्र करते थे।

Remember:
Hunter → Animals and birds
Gatherer → Plants, fruits, roots, seeds and other resources

3. Why Did Early People Move From Place to Place?

English

Early humans often moved because food and water were not available equally throughout the year.

If animals moved to another area, hunters might follow them. If a particular plant or fruit became available in another season, people could move towards that area.

हिंदी

प्रारंभिक लोग एक स्थान पर हमेशा नहीं रहते थे क्योंकि भोजन और पानी हर जगह तथा हर मौसम में समान रूप से उपलब्ध नहीं होते थे।

यदि जानवर किसी दूसरे क्षेत्र में चले जाते थे तो शिकारी उनका पीछा कर सकते थे। इसी तरह किसी मौसम में मिलने वाले फल या पौधे की तलाश में भी वे स्थान बदलते थे।

🌳 Example: If a forest does not provide enough food during one season, a group may move towards another area where water, animals or edible plants are available.

5. Stone Tools

English

Stone was one of the important materials used by early humans. Different types of tools could be made for different purposes.

🪨 Cutting Tools

Used for cutting plants, meat and other materials.

🏹 Hunting Tools

Tools helped people hunt animals and obtain food.

🔨 Processing Tools

Used to break, scrape or process materials.

🧰 Multi-purpose Tools

Some tools could be used for more than one activity.

हिंदी

पत्थर प्रारंभिक मानवों द्वारा उपयोग की जाने वाली महत्वपूर्ण सामग्री था। इससे काटने, शिकार करने और अन्य कार्यों के लिए अलग-अलग प्रकार के औजार बनाए जाते थे।

⭐ UPSC Link: Stone tools are archaeological evidence because they can help historians and archaeologists reconstruct aspects of early human life.

6. Discovery and Use of Fire

English

Fire was extremely important in the development of human life. It could provide warmth, help in cooking and offer protection from animals.

हिंदी

आग मानव जीवन के विकास में बहुत महत्वपूर्ण थी। इसका उपयोग भोजन पकाने, ठंड से बचने और जंगली जानवरों से सुरक्षा जैसे कार्यों में किया जा सकता था।

🔥 Example: Cooking food could make some foods easier to eat and digest.

7. Caves and Rock Shelters

Caves and rock shelters could provide protection from rain, heat, cold and wild animals.

Some caves and rock shelters also contain paintings and other evidence that help us study the lives and activities of prehistoric people.

हिंदी

गुफाएँ और शैलाश्रय बारिश, गर्मी, ठंड तथा जंगली जानवरों से सुरक्षा प्रदान कर सकते थे। कुछ स्थानों पर मिले चित्र और अन्य पुरातात्त्विक साक्ष्य प्रारंभिक मानव जीवन को समझने में सहायता करते हैं।

8. Bhimbetka — Important Example

Bhimbetka in present-day Madhya Pradesh is an important archaeological site associated with prehistoric rock shelters and paintings.

The site provides evidence useful for understanding aspects of prehistoric human activity.

⭐ UPSC Point:
Bhimbetka → Madhya Pradesh → Rock shelters → Prehistoric paintings

हिंदी

10. Beginning of Food Production

English

Over time, some communities began to produce food by growing plants and keeping animals. This was a major change in human history.

Food production could encourage people to stay in one area for longer periods, although the transition was gradual and varied across regions.

हिंदी

समय के साथ कुछ मानव समुदायों ने पौधे उगाना और पशुओं को पालना शुरू किया। यह मानव इतिहास में एक महत्वपूर्ण परिवर्तन था।

⭐ Key Concept: Food production is associated with the development of agriculture and animal domestication.

15. 📝 10 Chapter Test Series

Each test contains questions with answers and explanations. Complete all 10 tests for full chapter practice.

Test 1 — Basic Concepts

1. Hunter-gatherers mainly obtained food from:

Correct Answer: B — Natural surroundings.
Hunter-gatherers depended directly on naturally available resources such as animals, plants, fruits, roots and seeds.

Test 2 — Movement

2. Why did many early humans move from place to place?

Correct Answer: A.
Availability of food, water, plants and animals influenced movement.

Test 3 — Tools

3. Which material was especially important for early tools?

A. Plastic
B. Stone
C. Steel
D. Aluminium

Correct Answer: B — Stone.
Stone was an important material for making several types of early tools.

Test 4 — Fire

4. Which was NOT a major use of fire for early humans?

A. Cooking
B. Warmth
C. Protection
D. Internet communication

Correct Answer: D.
Internet communication is a modern technology and not a use of fire.

Test 5 — Bhimbetka

5. Bhimbetka is located in:

A. Madhya Pradesh
B. Punjab
C. Assam
D. Kerala

Correct Answer: A — Madhya Pradesh.
Bhimbetka is an important prehistoric archaeological site in Madhya Pradesh.

Test 6 — Archaeology

6. Archaeologists study which evidence to understand prehistoric life?

A. Only modern newspapers
B. Tools, shelters, paintings and other material remains
C. Television programmes
D. Modern websites

Correct Answer: B.
Material remains are important sources for reconstructing prehistoric life.

Test 7 — Food Production

7. Food production includes:

A. Growing plants and keeping animals
B. Only hunting
C. Only gathering
D. Manufacturing cars

Correct Answer: A.
Food production includes cultivation of plants and domestication/ keeping of animals.

Test 8 — Conceptual

8. Which development could encourage longer stays in one place?

A. Food production
B. Random movement
C. Lack of water
D. Absence of food

Correct Answer: A — Food production.
Cultivation and animal keeping could provide a more regular local food supply and support longer settlements.

Test 9 — UPSC Foundation

9. Which statement is correct?

A. All early humans lived permanently in cities.
B. Early human societies were completely independent of nature.
C. Natural resource availability influenced human movement.
D. Stone tools were never used.

Correct Answer: C.
Availability of natural resources was an important factor influencing the movement and survival strategies of early communities.

Test 10 — Final Revision

10. Which sequence best represents a broad development in human history?

A. Hunting-gathering → tool development → food production
B. Internet → agriculture → stone tools
C. Factories → hunting → stone tools
D. Cities → stone tools → hunting

Correct Answer: A.
This is a simplified learning sequence. Human development was gradual and differed across regions and communities.
