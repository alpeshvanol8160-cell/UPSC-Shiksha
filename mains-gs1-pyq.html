📚 GS-I Analytical Practice

Use these prompts to practise analytical Mains answers from History, Society and Geography.

Practice Scenario:

A rapidly growing city is experiencing population pressure, housing shortages, traffic congestion and stress on water resources. At the same time, migration is creating new economic opportunities.

  • Identify the major geographical factors involved.
  • Explain the social consequences of rapid urbanisation.
  • Discuss opportunities created by migration.
  • Suggest sustainable planning measures.
Suggested Answer Approach:
  • Begin with a brief introduction.
  • Analyse economic, social and geographical dimensions.
  • Use examples and relevant data where possible.
  • Discuss both opportunities and challenges.
  • End with practical and sustainable solutions.

🎯 GS-I Preparation Strategy

  • Build strong fundamentals in Indian History, World History, Geography, Society and Art & Culture.
  • Connect static topics with current developments.
  • Use maps, timelines and diagrams wherever relevant.
  • Practise analytical answers rather than only memorising facts.
  • For History, focus on causes, consequences, continuity and change.
  • For Geography, connect physical processes with human and economic activities.
  • For Society, discuss social changes using balanced and contemporary examples.

📚 Major GS-I Areas

🏛️ Indian History

Ancient, Medieval and Modern India, freedom struggle and post-independence developments.

🌍 World History

Industrial Revolution, world wars, colonisation, nationalism and political transformations.

👥 Indian Society

Diversity, urbanisation, migration, social change and population issues.

🗺️ Geography

Physical geography, resources, climate, agriculture, industries and human geography.

🎨 Art & Culture

Architecture, literature, painting, religious traditions and cultural heritage.

🤝 Social Issues

Women, vulnerable groups, poverty, education, health and social empowerment.
