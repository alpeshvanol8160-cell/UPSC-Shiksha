🎯 GS-I PYQ Strategy

1️⃣ Read the question carefully and identify the demand.

2️⃣ Divide the answer into Introduction, Body and Conclusion.

3️⃣ Use examples, facts, maps and relevant concepts wherever appropriate.

4️⃣ For History and Society, connect static concepts with suitable examples.

5️⃣ For Geography, use diagrams or maps where they add value.

6️⃣ Practice writing within the required word limit.

7️⃣ Use official UPSC papers as the reference for actual PYQs.
