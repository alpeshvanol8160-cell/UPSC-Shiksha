🎯 10. How to Prepare History

Step 1 — Build the Timeline

First understand the chronology of major events, kingdoms, movements and important developments.

Step 2 — Connect Causes & Consequences

Do not simply memorize events. Understand why an event happened and what happened afterwards.

Step 3 — Make Short Notes

Prepare concise notes for important dates, personalities, movements, acts and cultural topics.

Step 4 — Solve PYQs

Practice previous year questions to understand UPSC's question pattern and improve revision.

📌 Remember: History preparation becomes easier when you combine chronology + concepts + maps + revision + PYQs.
