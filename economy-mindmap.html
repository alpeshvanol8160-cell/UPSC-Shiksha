📚 Economy Basics & National Income

💵 GDP

Gross Domestic Product measures the value of final goods and services produced within an economy during a specified period.

📊 GVA

Gross Value Added measures value generated by producers after accounting for intermediate consumption.

💰 National Income

Study national income concepts, sectoral contribution, per capita income and related indicators.

Concept What to Understand
GDP Production within domestic territory.
GVA Value added by economic activities/sectors.
Per Capita Income Income measure relative to population.
Real vs Nominal Real values adjust for price changes; nominal values use current prices.
Economic Sectors Primary, secondary and tertiary activities.
📈 Economic Growth, Development & Employment

📈 Economic Growth

Increase in economic output over time.

🌱 Economic Development

Broader improvement in income, capabilities, living standards and opportunities.

👷 Employment

Study unemployment, labour force participation, job creation and structural transformation.

⭐ IMPORTANT: Growth and development are related but not identical. UPSC answers should connect growth with inclusion, employment, human capital and sustainability.
🔥 Inflation & Monetary Economy

📈 Inflation

Persistent increase in the general price level reducing purchasing power.

🏦 Monetary Policy

Central bank measures affecting liquidity, interest rates and monetary conditions.

💹 Interest Rates

Interest rates influence borrowing, investment, consumption and inflationary conditions.

Term UPSC Understanding
Repo Rate Policy rate associated with RBI's lending to banks under the relevant framework.
CRR Cash reserve requirement maintained by banks with the RBI.
SLR Requirement relating to liquid assets maintained by banks.
Inflation General rise in prices over time.
Deflation Sustained decline in general price levels.
⭐⭐⭐ VERY IMPORTANT: Inflation + Monetary Policy + RBI are major areas for conceptual Prelims questions and analytical Mains answers.
🏦 Banking & Financial System

🏦 RBI

India's central bank with important monetary, banking and financial-system functions.

🏛️ Commercial Banks

Mobilise deposits and provide credit and other financial services.

⚠️ NPAs

Non-performing assets indicate stressed bank credit and require careful analysis.

📱 Digital Payments

UPI and digital financial systems have expanded payment access and formalisation.

👥 Financial Inclusion

Focus on access to banking, credit, insurance, pensions and digital finance.

💳 FinTech

Technology-driven financial services, digital lending and payment innovations.

💰 Government Budget & Fiscal Policy
Concept Meaning / Focus
Revenue Receipts Government receipts that generally do not create liabilities or reduce assets in the same way as capital transactions.
Capital Receipts Receipts involving liabilities or reduction of assets.
Fiscal Deficit Overall financing requirement arising when total expenditure exceeds relevant non-borrowing receipts.
Revenue Deficit Revenue expenditure exceeding revenue receipts.
Public Debt Government borrowing and associated liabilities.
Taxation Direct and indirect taxes are major sources of government revenue.
⭐⭐⭐ VERY IMPORTANT: Budget + Fiscal Deficit + Taxation + Public Debt should be revised together.
🌾 Agriculture & Rural Economy

🌾 Cropping Patterns

Study Kharif, Rabi and Zaid seasons, regional crop patterns and diversification.

💧 Irrigation

Canals, wells, tube wells, micro-irrigation and water-use efficiency.

🚜 Farm Mechanisation

Technology, productivity, labour efficiency and access to farm machinery.

🌱 Sustainable Agriculture

Soil health, water conservation, climate resilience and diversification.

📱 Agri Technology

Digital platforms, precision agriculture, market information and e-technology.

🐄 Animal Husbandry

Livestock, dairy, fisheries and allied activities contribute to rural livelihoods.

⭐⭐⭐ VERY IMPORTANT: Agriculture questions should be connected with water, markets, technology, climate change, farm incomes and food security.
🍚 MSP, Subsidies, PDS & Food Security
Topic What to Study
MSP Minimum Support Price and its role in agricultural price support.
Farm Subsidies Direct and indirect subsidies and their economic implications.
PDS Public Distribution System and foodgrain distribution.
Buffer Stocks Foodgrain stocks maintained for food-security and market-management purposes.
Food Security Availability, access, utilisation and stability of food.
Food Processing Value addition, supply chains, storage, logistics and market access.

🟢 Prelims

MSP, PDS, subsidies, foodgrain systems, agricultural terminology and institutions.

🟠 Mains

Food security, farm income, agricultural markets, subsidy reform, storage and supply chains.

🌐 External Sector

🌍 Balance of Payments

Records economic transactions between residents of an economy and the rest of the world.

💱 Exchange Rate

Price of one currency in terms of another; important for trade and capital flows.

💵 Forex Reserves

Foreign exchange assets held by the monetary authority for external stability and related purposes.

🏢 FDI

Foreign Direct Investment involves investment with a lasting interest/control dimension.

📊 FPI

Foreign Portfolio Investment involves investment in financial assets without the same control dimension.

🌐 WTO

International organisation dealing with rules of global trade.

🏭 Industry, Liberalisation & Manufacturing

🏭 Manufacturing

Industrial production, productivity, employment and value chains.

📉 Liberalisation

Economic reforms aimed at reducing certain controls and increasing market orientation.

🌐 Globalisation

Greater integration of production, trade, finance and technology.

🤝 PPP

Public-Private Partnership models used in infrastructure and public service projects.

📦 MSMEs

Important for employment, entrepreneurship, regional development and supply chains.

🚢 Logistics

Transport, warehousing, ports and supply-chain efficiency influence industrial competitiveness.

👥 Poverty, Employment & Inclusive Growth

📉 Poverty

Study poverty measurement, multidimensional poverty and factors affecting deprivation.

👷 Unemployment

Open unemployment, disguised unemployment, underemployment and labour-market issues.

🎓 Human Capital

Education, health, skills and productivity form important components of human capital.

👥 Inclusive Growth

Growth that expands economic opportunities and access across different sections of society.

👩 Women & Economy

Female labour-force participation, skills, entrepreneurship and care economy.

👨‍💻 Skill Development

Skills improve employability, productivity and adaptation to changing labour markets.

🛣️ Infrastructure & Investment

⚡ Energy

Electricity generation, transmission, renewable energy and energy security.

🛣️ Roads

Road connectivity supports mobility, trade and regional development.

🚆 Railways

Major transport infrastructure supporting passenger and freight movement.

⚓ Ports

Critical nodes for international trade and logistics.

✈️ Airports

Air connectivity supports trade, tourism, business and regional integration.

💰 Investment Models

Understand public investment, private investment and PPP models.

🌱 Green Economy & Sustainable Development

☀️ Renewable Energy

Solar, wind, hydropower and other low-carbon energy sources.

🌱 Sustainable Development

Balancing economic development, social welfare and environmental protection.

♻️ Circular Economy

Reduce waste, reuse resources and improve resource efficiency.

💨 Carbon Economy

Understand emissions, mitigation, carbon markets and transition challenges.
