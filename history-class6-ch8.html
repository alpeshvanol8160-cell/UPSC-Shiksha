🇬🇧 English Explanation

1. The Mauryan Empire

The Mauryan Empire was one of the largest political powers in ancient India. It emerged in the late fourth century BCE and gradually expanded over a large geographical area.

Chandragupta Maurya is traditionally regarded as the founder of the Mauryan dynasty. Later rulers expanded and consolidated Mauryan power.

Key Idea: A large empire required military power as well as administration, revenue collection, officials and communication systems.

2. Who Was Ashoka?

Ashoka was one of the most famous rulers of the Mauryan dynasty. He ruled during the third century BCE.

He is remembered especially for his inscriptions, the Kalinga War and his policy of dhamma.

👑 Mauryan Rule

Ashoka inherited a powerful empire and became one of its most important rulers.

⚔️ Expansion

Like other rulers, Ashoka was involved in military expansion.

⚔️ Kalinga War

The conquest of Kalinga became a major turning point in his reign.

🕊️ Dhamma

After the war, Ashoka gave greater emphasis to ethical conduct and social harmony.

⚔️ 3. The Kalinga War

Kalinga was an important region on the eastern coast of the Indian subcontinent. Ashoka conquered Kalinga in a major military campaign.

According to Ashoka's own inscriptions, the war caused extensive suffering and loss of life.

⚔️
Kalinga

War → Suffering → Reflection → Greater emphasis on Dhamma

Important Historical Point: Ashoka did not simply stop being a ruler after the Kalinga War. Instead, he continued governing while giving greater importance to ethical conduct and welfare.

Why Is Kalinga Important?

  • It was a major military campaign of Ashoka.
  • Ashoka's inscriptions describe the suffering caused by the war.
  • The war is associated with a major change in the ruler's public policy.
  • It provides an important example of how historical sources can reveal a ruler's ideas.

🕊️ 4. Ashoka's Dhamma

After the Kalinga War, Ashoka placed greater emphasis on dhamma. The term referred to a broad set of ethical principles and social values.

Dhamma was not simply a military policy or a taxation system. It focused on behaviour, relationships and social harmony.

Important Values Associated with Dhamma

  • Respect for parents and elders
  • Kindness towards living beings
  • Good treatment of servants and workers
  • Respect for different groups
  • Self-control and moderation
  • Peaceful social relations
UPSC Concept: Ashoka's dhamma should not simply be equated with Buddhism. It was presented as a broad ethical and social policy.

🤝 5. Respect for Different Groups

Ashoka's messages encouraged people to behave respectfully towards people with different beliefs and practices.

This idea is important because the Mauryan Empire included many regions, communities and traditions.

Example: A large empire containing different communities could face social tensions. Encouraging mutual respect could help promote social stability.

🪨 6. Ashoka's Inscriptions

One of the most important sources for studying Ashoka is his inscriptions. They were engraved on rocks and pillars in different parts of the empire.

📜 Historical Evidence

The inscriptions contain messages related to dhamma, administration, welfare and the conduct expected from people.

Source-Based Learning: Ashoka's inscriptions are especially valuable because they provide contemporary evidence connected directly with his reign.

🔤 7. Languages and Scripts

Ashoka's inscriptions were written in different languages and scripts depending on the region.

Most inscriptions used scripts that were intended to communicate messages to people in different parts of the empire.

Important: Historians study the language, script, location and content of inscriptions to understand the Mauryan period.

🏛️ 8. How Was the Empire Governed?

A huge empire could not be managed by the ruler alone. Officials and administrative arrangements were necessary.

System Importance
👨‍💼 Officials Helped carry out administrative decisions.
🪙 Revenue Provided resources for the state.
⚔️ Army Protected territory and supported political authority.
🛣️ Communication Helped connect different parts of the empire.
🏛️ Centres of Power Helped coordinate administration.

🌱 9. Welfare and Public Works

Ashoka's inscriptions also mention concern for the welfare of people and animals.

His messages referred to measures related to roads, shade, wells and other welfare-oriented activities.

🧠 10. Why Are Ashoka's Inscriptions Important?

Historical knowledge is based on evidence. Ashoka's inscriptions allow historians to examine what the ruler himself wanted to communicate.

Evidence Possible Information
Inscriptions Ashoka's messages, policies and ethical ideas.
Archaeology Buildings, settlements and material remains.
Literary Sources Religious, social and political traditions.
Foreign Accounts Observations from visitors or writers from outside the region.
Historical Method: No single source necessarily gives the complete picture. Historians compare different kinds of evidence.

🇮🇳 हिंदी में विस्तृत व्याख्या

1. मौर्य साम्राज्य

मौर्य साम्राज्य प्राचीन भारत की सबसे महत्वपूर्ण राजनीतिक शक्तियों में से एक था। इसका विकास चौथी शताब्दी ईसा पूर्व के अंतिम भाग में हुआ।

चंद्रगुप्त मौर्य को मौर्य वंश का संस्थापक माना जाता है। बाद के शासकों ने मौर्य शक्ति को और विस्तृत किया।

2. अशोक कौन थे?

अशोक मौर्य वंश के प्रसिद्ध शासक थे। उन्होंने तीसरी शताब्दी ईसा पूर्व में शासन किया।

अशोक को विशेष रूप से कलिंग युद्ध, उनके अभिलेखों और धम्म की नीति के लिए याद किया जाता है।

3. कलिंग युद्ध

कलिंग पूर्वी तट का एक महत्वपूर्ण क्षेत्र था। अशोक ने कलिंग पर विजय प्राप्त करने के लिए युद्ध किया।

अशोक के अभिलेखों के अनुसार युद्ध के कारण बहुत अधिक पीड़ा और जनहानि हुई। इस अनुभव ने अशोक की सार्वजनिक नीति पर गहरा प्रभाव डाला।

ध्यान दें: अशोक ने युद्ध के बाद शासन छोड़ नहीं दिया था। उन्होंने शासन जारी रखा, लेकिन नैतिक आचरण और धम्म पर अधिक जोर दिया।

4. अशोक का धम्म

अशोक का धम्म व्यापक नैतिक और सामाजिक मूल्यों से संबंधित था। इसमें माता-पिता और बड़ों का सम्मान, जीवों के प्रति दया, संयम और विभिन्न समूहों के प्रति सम्मान जैसे विचार शामिल थे।

महत्वपूर्ण: धम्म को केवल बौद्ध धर्म का दूसरा नाम नहीं माना जाना चाहिए। यह व्यापक नैतिक और सामाजिक नीति थी।

5. अशोक के अभिलेख

अशोक ने अपने संदेशों को चट्टानों और स्तंभों पर खुदवाया। इन्हें अभिलेख कहा जाता है।

ये अभिलेख इतिहासकारों के लिए बहुत महत्वपूर्ण स्रोत हैं क्योंकि इनके माध्यम से अशोक के विचारों और नीतियों के बारे में जानकारी मिलती है।

6. प्रशासन

इतने बड़े साम्राज्य को चलाने के लिए अधिकारियों, कर व्यवस्था, सेना और संचार व्यवस्था की आवश्यकता थी।

7. जनता का कल्याण

अशोक के संदेशों में लोगों और पशुओं के कल्याण की चिंता दिखाई देती है। सड़कों, कुओं, छाया और अन्य सुविधाओं से संबंधित कार्यों का भी उल्लेख मिलता है।

8. इतिहासकारों के लिए अभिलेखों का महत्व

अभिलेख किसी समय के शासक के विचारों और नीतियों को समझने का महत्वपूर्ण स्रोत होते हैं। लेकिन इतिहासकार अन्य स्रोतों के साथ उनकी तुलना भी करते हैं।
