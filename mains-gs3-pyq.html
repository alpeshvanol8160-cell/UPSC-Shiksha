📚 GS-III Case Study Practice

Case-study practice helps in applying concepts from economy, environment, technology, disaster management and internal security.

Case Study:

A district is facing repeated floods. The administration has limited funds and must decide how to balance immediate relief with long-term disaster-resilient infrastructure.

  • Identify the major stakeholders.
  • Explain the immediate priorities.
  • Suggest long-term measures.
  • Explain how technology can support disaster preparedness.
Suggested Approach:
  • Identify facts and risks first.
  • Prioritise protection of life and essential services.
  • Use transparent and evidence-based planning.
  • Combine structural and non-structural measures.
  • Include community participation.

🎯 GS-III Preparation Strategy

  • Build strong concepts in Economy, Agriculture, Environment, Science & Technology and Security.
  • Connect static concepts with current affairs.
  • Use diagrams, flowcharts and examples in Mains answers.
  • Practice previous-year questions regularly.
  • For 10-marker questions, focus on concise and structured answers.
  • For 15-marker questions, provide multiple dimensions, examples and a balanced conclusion.
  • Revise government schemes, institutions and important reports.
